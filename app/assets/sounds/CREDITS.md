# Sound credits

The pick/ban show's sound cues (`intro`, `pick`, `ban`, `ban-down`, `decider`) are original mixes made for it: each file layers, edits and masters the sounds below with synthesized layers. `cinematic/` holds the Cinematic pack and `clean/` the Clean pack, which plays `cinematic/decider.mp3` for its decider.
